## Excluded from the statistics — different basis

A figure on a different basis is not in the same distribution. These are reported in the board table but kept out of the medians and ranges above rather than averaged into them.

- **KREF — Management fee**: pct of adjusted equity (row reference: assets)
- **KREF — NAV per share**: class: common; measure: book value per share (row reference: nav_per_share)
- **KREF — Leverage (D/E), regulatory**: recourse debt to equity (row reference: gross_debt_to_equity)
- **KREF — Leverage (D/E), economic**: total liabilities ex securitisation to equity (row reference: total_liabilities_to_equity)

## Not ranked

A rank implies better and worse. For NAV per share and Leverage (D/E), regulatory and Leverage (D/E), economic that is not defined — a NAV per share is a share price, not a quality, and a leverage ratio is a risk posture, not a score. Both are shown in order without a ranking claim.
